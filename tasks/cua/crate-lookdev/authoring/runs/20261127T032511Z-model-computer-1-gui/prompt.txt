## What to build

Model the crate in Blender and render it. Use the GUI; the deliverable is a
scene a colleague can keep editing, not a screenshot.

1. Geometry, in metres:
   - five body panels `crate_front`, `crate_back`, `crate_left`, `crate_right`,
     `crate_bottom`, each exactly 20 mm thick, outer envelope 800 × 600 × 600 mm;
   - a lid `crate_lid`, hinged on the rear top edge, opening 0–95°, driven by a
     real pivot (an empty or the lid's own origin parented to one) — not a
     translate or a shape key;
   - eight corner brackets `bracket_01` … `bracket_08`, 60 × 60 × 2 mm, one per
     vertical corner, top and bottom, standing proud of the timber;
   - a stencil object `stencil_text` on the front face, white worn paint.
2. Edges are chamfered (bevel ≥ 2 mm). Panels and brackets are separate objects
   with real thickness; do not fake thickness with a texture.
3. Materials: weathered oak with 2K basecolor + roughness + normal maps (all
   packed into the .blend), rusted iron brackets with metallic 1.0, and stencil
   paint whose lettering is broken up by wear rather than a crisp new font.
4. Lights, cameras and the HDRI live in the file; every image is packed. The
   file must open and render after `/app/materials` is removed.

## How it is checked

All of these must hold: the file reopens in Blender; all named objects exist;
outer size 800 × 600 × 600 mm ±5 mm; every panel 20 mm ±2 mm; the lid pivots at
the rear top edge and does not intersect the body at 0°, 45° and 95°; bevels
≥ 2 mm; three packed maps ≥ 2048 px wired to basecolor / roughness / normal;
wood roughness standard deviation ≥ 0.05; bracket metallic ≥ 0.9; the four
renders exist at the stated resolutions from the stated cameras; the manifest
covers the HDRI with a licence.

Two anti-cheat checks run against the delivered files. The scene is re-rendered
from `cam_front` and compared with your `render_front.png` (they must match), and
the lid is then rotated 60° and rendered again (the image must change).

Each delivered view/state is independently re-rendered from its specified
camera. It must match in every RGB channel (SSIM >= 0.90) and in image
structure (relative RGB gradient error after a 2-pixel Gaussian blur <= 0.60).
Agreement on a large uniform background alone is insufficient. Compositing
or a video sequence cannot substitute for the delivered 3D scene.
