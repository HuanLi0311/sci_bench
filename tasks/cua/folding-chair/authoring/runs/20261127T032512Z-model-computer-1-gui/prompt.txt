design pack for a folding chair; `/app/out` is where you deliver.

## What to build

Model the chair in metres, at millimetres from the drawing.

1. Parts: `seat`, `back`, `front_leg_l`, `front_leg_r`, `rear_leg_l`,
   `rear_leg_r`, and four hinges `hinge_01` … `hinge_04` at the coordinates in
   the table.
2. The two legs on each side form a scissor pinned through `hinge_01` /
   `hinge_02`; the seat pivots on `hinge_03` and the back on `hinge_04`. Bars
   are parented to the hinge empties, so the joint stays coincident when the
   hinge rotates. Do not use shape keys or baked vertex animation.
3. Unfolded (frame 0): 620 × 580 × 820 mm, seat top at 450 mm. Folded (frame
   60): a 90 mm pack. Animate 0–60 at 24 fps with the hinges as the drivers.
4. No part may pass through another at any frame: sample the fold every two
   frames. Contact at a pinned joint is fine; penetration elsewhere is not.
5. At most 120k triangles. Textures packed.

## How it is checked

The file must reopen; all ten parts/hinges exist; hinges within 2 mm of the
layout; unfolded size 620 × 580 × 820 ±5 mm; folded pack ≤ 95 mm; 0–60 frames
at 24 fps; no interpenetration across 31 sampled poses; each leg's centroid
within 20 mm of its hinge axis; four 1200 × 1200 renders from `cam_states`;
textures packed; manifest complete.

Anti-cheat: the scene is re-rendered at frames 0 and 30 from `cam_states`. The
delivered unfolded render must match the frame-0 re-render (SSIM ≥ 0.90) and
frame 30 must differ from frame 0 (SSIM ≤ 0.98).

Each delivered view/state is independently re-rendered from its specified
camera. It must match in every RGB channel (SSIM >= 0.90) and in image
structure (relative RGB gradient error after a 2-pixel Gaussian blur <= 0.60).
Agreement on a large uniform background alone is insufficient. Compositing
or a video sequence cannot substitute for the delivered 3D scene.
